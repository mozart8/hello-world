# Cheese Pizza Recipe

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast (1 packet)
- 1 cup warm water (110°F)
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp olive oil

### Sauce
- 1 can (15 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Toppings
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Dough
1. Dissolve sugar in warm water, then sprinkle yeast over it. Let sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy.
2. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt. Add the yeast mixture and olive oil.
3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. Place dough in an oiled bowl, cover, and let rise for 1 hour until doubled in size.

### 2. Make the Sauce
1. Heat a small saucepan over medium heat with a drizzle of olive oil.
2. Saute garlic for 1 minute, then add crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper.
3. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

### 3. Assemble and Bake
1. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven while preheating.
2. Punch down the risen dough and roll it out to a 12-inch circle on a floured surface.
3. Transfer dough to a greased baking sheet or pizza stone.
4. Spread sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border.
5. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
6. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.

## Serves
4–6 people
